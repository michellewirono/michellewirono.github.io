Heng Heng

Brand Identity & Social Campaign

A refreshed brand identity for Heng Heng, a Teochew fishball noodle brand, extending its visual system across logo concepts, menu design, and social campaign assets.

Identity Narrative

Heng Heng serves Teochew fishball noodles. Its identity brings the bowl, fish, and chopsticks together in a memorable mark, supported by a warm red-and-cream palette.

Problem

Create a distinctive visual identity that connects the brand to its Teochew fishball noodle offering and works across menu and social formats.

Solution

Develop a cohesive logo system and carry it into menu and social campaign layouts, keeping the brand recognizable across each touchpoint.

Logomark

The mark combines a bowl, fish, and chopsticks to make the food the focus. Supporting wordmarks and color usage extend the identity across different layouts.

Programs Used

Adobe Photoshop, After Effects, Illustrator, and Figma
