About

Michelle Wirono is an Indonesian-born Graphic Designer based in Los Angeles, holding a valedictorian BFA in Illustration from Columbus College of Art & Design and an MBA in Marketing.

Blending a concept art background with strategic brand thinking, she specializes in visual identity, packaging, and digital design—creating clear, engaging work where strong storytelling meets usability.

She is a café hopper, a focaccia and cookie baker, a thrift-flip seamstress, and an aspiring future cat café owner.
